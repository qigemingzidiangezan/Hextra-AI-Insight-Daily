## **AI Daily Brief**
Meta's comprehensive AI strategy is the spotlight of today's AI landscape. At its annual Connect conference, the company made it clear it's going all-in on its AI agent, **Muse**. It also unveiled a series of hardware innovations centered around Muse, including integration into AI glasses, the launch of camera-free AI glasses, and a standalone **Muse Charm** wearable device, while enhancing Muse's capabilities to support video chat. Other tech giants are also rolling out AI feature updates: **ChatGPT** mobile app added voice agent functionalities, and **YouTube Music** introduced conversational AI search. On the application front, AI biotech company **Enveda** secured massive funding to accelerate AI drug discovery. Furthermore, **Anthropic's** biology lab announced significant discoveries, emphasizing human oversight in its research. Meanwhile, public concerns about AI and support for regulation continue. 💡
