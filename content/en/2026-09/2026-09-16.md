## **AI Content Summary**
Today's AI landscape is a whirlwind of complexity and controversy. Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang is stirring the pot, publicly opposing AI regulation. Huang argues that AI is fundamentally software and hardware, and safety should be self-regulated by manufacturers. Meanwhile, big players like OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google are deep in discussions about AI safety, though outsiders are debating if these talks are truly about "safety protocols" or just "industry monopolization." Shifting gears, AI data centers are rapidly expanding worldwide, causing headaches with massive energy consumption (US data center natural gas consumption alone is projected to surpass Germany and Japan combined by 2035!), significant environmental impact, and public pushback, often clashing with urban development. On the product front, Meta is making big moves, launching AI subscription services and using AI agents to streamline WhatsApp business setups. And just a friendly reminder, the industry is also seeing an "AI project graveyard" phenomenon, a stark reminder that AI development isn't always smooth sailing.
