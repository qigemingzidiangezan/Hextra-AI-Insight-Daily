## AI Daily Buzz
The AI world is buzzing with activity today! **AfterQuery**, an AI model training startup, has zoomed to unicorn status at an astonishing pace, becoming Y Combinator's fastest-ever unicorn with a $3.2 billion valuation. **OpenAI** is gearing up to launch its cybersecurity-critical large model, **Astra**, after some delays due to earlier security concerns.
