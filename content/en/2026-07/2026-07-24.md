## **AI Product and Feature Updates**

1.  **AMD** is throwing down the gauntlet to Nvidia with its new Helios AI rack-scale system, slated to ship to customers later this year.
    [Original Link](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/23/amd-takes-on-nvidia-with-its-helios-ai-rack-scale-system/)

2.  **Anthropic** has supercharged Claude's voice mode, now supporting more powerful models like Opus and Sonnet. This upgrade allows it to tackle complex tasks such as rescheduling meetings and drafting emails.
    [Original Link](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/23/anthropic-updates-claude-voice-mode-with-more-capable-models/)
    [Original Link](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/970065/anthropic-voice-mode-claude-opus-sonnet-haiku-ai)

    [Original Link](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/23/aegisai-founded-by-former-google-security-execs-lands-36m-to-stop-ai-driven-spear-phishing/)

4.  **Runway** has unveiled its AI Model Router (Media Router), offering developers in the generative media space a powerful tool. This router can automatically pick the optimal image, video, or audio generation model based on priorities like quality, speed, or cost.
    [Original Link](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/23/runway-bets-on-ai-model-routing-as-generative-media-gets-crowded/)

5.  **OpenAI** has made ChatGPT Health available to all U.S. users. This means users can now integrate their personal health data from services like Apple Health, Function, and MyFitnessPal.
    [Original Link](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/23/openai-makes-chatgpt-health-available-to-all-u-s-users/)
    [Original Link](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/970115/openai-chatgpt-health-launch-claims)

6.  **Amazon** has rolled out an update for Alexa Plus, using AI to boost its capability to handle more complex commands and seamlessly connect with smart home devices.
    [Original Link](https://www.theverge.com/tech/970399/amazon-alexa-plus-ai-update-smart-home-devices)
