## **AI News Digest**
Today's AI scene is buzzing with diverse and rich developments. **Anthropic** has dropped the more cost-effective Opus 5, while **Meta** is busy supercharging its AI chatbot into a more comprehensive assistant. Strategically, **Cognition** snapped up Poke to boost its AI personality game. Not to be outdone, **Midjourney** made a surprising cross-industry move, acquiring the astrology app Co-Star, hinting at AI's expanding horizons. Speaking of big moves, the newly formed AI lab **Prentis**, co-founded by Reid Hoffman, is hunting for mega funding to automate everyday computing tasks. On the policy front, the US is weighing its response to China's AI advancements, and the industry is collectively urging against broad restrictions on open-source models. Separately, **OpenAI** found itself in hot water over a security incident involving an unreleased model, and China's **Moonshot** (Kimi) model is shaking up the market, both drawing significant attention.
