## **AI Product & Feature Updates**
*   **Google Gemini:** now lets users remove **visible watermarks** from AI-generated content (images, videos, music), though invisible watermarks will stick around. ✅ [Original Link](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/14/google-will-now-allow-users-to-remove-visible-watermark-from-its-ai-generations/) / [Original Link](https://www.theverge.com/tech/980416/google-gemini-ai-watermarks-removal)
*   **Meta (Glimmer):** just launched **Glimmer**, an **open-source weight AI model** anyone can download and run on their own hardware. This is a neat contrast to their more locked-down API model, Muse Spark. And yep, Mark Zuckerberg is still championing "AI for everyone." 🌐 [Original Link](https://techcrunch.com/video/does-mark-zuckerberg-really-believe-ai-is-for-everyone/) / [Original Link](https://techcrunch.com/podcast/metas-open-ai-and-a-250m-deal-gone-very-wrong/)
*   **OpenAI (GPT-5.6 Sol):** has rolled out a preview of the "**Ultrafast**" mode for **GPT-5.6 Sol**, boosting its speed by a whopping 14 times! This is a clear move to attract enterprise users. ⚡ [Original Link](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/13/openai-introduces-ultrafast-a-new-mode-that-makes-gpt-5-6-sol-work-at-14x-the-speed/)
*   **Apple (China Custom AI Model):** is reportedly teaming up with **Alibaba** to train a **custom AI model** specifically for the Chinese market. Talk about a rare cross-border collab! 🤝 [Original Link](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/980160/apple-intelligence-china-custom-ai-model-alibaba)
*   **Writer:** has launched a new AI model, built on Z.ai's open-source GLM-5.2. They've also upgraded their "**harness**" to smartly manage **token costs**, which means more affordable deployments. 💰 [Original Link](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/13/writer-introduces-new-ai-model-and-upgraded-harness-to-contain-token-costs/)
*   **Microsoft Copilot:** is saying goodbye to its Clippy-style yellow cartoon mascot, **Mico**, which has officially been "retired." 👋 [Original Link](https://www.theverge.com/tech/979871/microsoft-copilot-mico-retired)
*   **Databricks:** just wrapped up a **$5 billion funding round**, pushing its valuation to a whopping $190 billion! This huge investment is all about tackling the massive costs of AI model development and deployment. 💰 [Original Link](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/13/databricks-wanted-to-raise-1b-investors-wanted-15b-it-settled-on-5b-at-a-190b-valuation/)
