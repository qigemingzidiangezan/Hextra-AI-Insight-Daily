## **AI Daily Brief**
Today's AI news is packed! We're seeing big headlines around AI infrastructure's environmental impact, model security concerns, and a slew of product innovations. Amazon's planned data center in Texas, for example, is causing a stir because its accompanying power plant could become the largest climate polluter in the U.S. Meanwhile, OpenAI, on the flip side, has hit the brakes on some internal activities due to worries that its Astra model might be capable of independently carrying out cyberattacks, underscoring the urgent need for AI safety and ethics discussions. On the product innovation front, OpenAI also snatched up presentation startup NextSlide, Rippling is now rolling out an AI Spend Console, Cloudflare just dropped Kitesurf—a browser tailor-made for AI agents—and both Airbnb and Roku have launched new AI-driven search functions and AI-generated content channels, respectively. This really shows how AI tech is diving deep into every industry.
