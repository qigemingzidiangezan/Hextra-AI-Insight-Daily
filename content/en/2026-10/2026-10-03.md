## **Your Daily AI Rundown**
Today's AI world is buzzing, with major shifts happening in both policy and product safety. First off, the **White House** has officially rebranded AI as 'super intelligence' and pushed tech giants to sign AI safety commitments – though consumers aren't quite sold on the new moniker yet. On the product side, **Meta** just open-sourced its Muse AI tech, looking to spark some cool new hardware innovations, while **OpenAI** dropped Dot, a versatile enterprise AI agent. Safety's a hot topic, too: **Apple** is tightening macOS disk access due to potential AI agent risks, and **Circuit Breaker Labs** is zeroing in on kids' psychological AI safety. Plus, under Sean Parker's guidance, **Stability AI** is making a big pivot, focusing its efforts squarely on music AI.

## **What's New in AI Products & Features**

1.  **Stability AI**: Under Sean Parker's lead, **Stability AI** is totally shaking things up, refocusing its entire business around music AI. This bold move has already snagged support and investments from the music industry and major record labels. 🎶 [Original Link](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/02/sean-parker-is-rebuilding-stability-ai-around-music/)

2.  **Apple macOS**: **Apple** is stepping up its game with macOS 'Full Disk Access' controls. Why? To tackle fresh risks from increasingly powerful AI agents that could potentially snoop on your files, messages, and browsing history. It's all about user protection! 🔒 [Original Link](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/02/apple-says-its-tightening-macos-full-disk-access-controls-due-to-new-risks-from-ai-agents/)

3.  **OpenAI Dot Agent**: **OpenAI** just dropped their new AI agent, Dot! This isn't just any enterprise software; Dot is also capable of handling everyday tasks for users, like ordering your dinner. Pretty neat, right? ✨ [Original Link](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1004096/openai-chatgpt-dots-hands-on-agent)

4.  **Circuit Breaker Labs**: **Circuit Breaker Labs** is on a mission to make AI safer for everyone, from kids to adults. They're tackling potential psychological harm from AI by creating "crash test dummies" – a super interesting approach! 🛡️ [Original Link](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/02/circuit-breaker-labs-hopes-to-make-ai-safer-for-your-kids-and-you/)

5.  **U.S. White House**: In a high-stakes meeting with top tech CEOs, the **U.S. White House** officially rebranded AI as 'super intelligence' and pushed for a 'morally binding' AI safety commitment. Here's the kicker: only 2% of consumers are actually buying into this new concept so far. Talk about a tough crowd! 🏛️ [Original Link](https://techcrunch.com/podcast/call-it-ai-call-it-super-intelligence-only-2-of-consumers-are-buying-it/)
