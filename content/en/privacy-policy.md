### Tracking Technologies & Cookies
**We** totally use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our service and stash away certain info. The tracking technologies we deploy include beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to gather and track info, plus jazz up and analyze our service. Here's a peek at what we might use:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file chilling on your device. You can tell your browser to give all Cookies the cold shoulder or to ping you when one's being sent. But heads up, if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to dive into some parts of our service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to wave goodbye to Cookies, our service might just go ahead and use 'em.
- **Web Beacons**: Some bits of our service and our emails might pack tiny electronic files called Web Beacons (also known as clear GIFs, pixel tags, and single-pixel GIFs). These bad boys let the Company, for example, count users who've visited certain pages or opened an email, and they're also handy for other related website stats (like tracking how popular a section is and making sure systems and servers are all good).

**Cookies** can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. When you're offline, Persistent Cookies hang around on your personal computer or mobile device, while Session Cookies get the boot the moment you shut down your web browser.

**We** fire up both Session and Persistent Cookies for these purposes:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super crucial for us to deliver the services available through the website and let you flex some of its features. They're key in authenticating users and keeping a lid on fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you're asking for just can't happen, and we only use 'em to hook you up with those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notification Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are all about figuring out if users have given the thumbs up to using Cookies on the website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you're cruising the website, like recalling your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of typing in your preferences every single time you hit up the website.

For the full scoop on the Cookies we use and your options regarding them, mosey on over to our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
