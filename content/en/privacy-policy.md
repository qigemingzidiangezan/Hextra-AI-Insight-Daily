### Tracking Tech & Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash away certain info. The tracking technologies we use include beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to gather and track info, and to help us tweak and analyze our Service. The tech we might use includes:

-   **[Cookies or Browser Cookies]**: A Cookie is a tiny file placed on your Device. You can tell your browser to totally refuse all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But hey, if you don't play along with Cookies, you might not be able to use certain parts of our Service. Unless you've adjusted your browser to say 'no thanks' to Cookies, our Service will likely be using 'em.
-   **[Web Beacons]**: Certain sections of our Service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files known as web beacons (also called clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These awesome files let the Company, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email. They're also used for other cool website stats, like checking how popular a specific section is and verifying system and server integrity.

Cookies can be either 'Persistent' or 'Session' Cookies. When you go offline, Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device. Session Cookies, on the other hand, vanish the moment you close your web browser.

We totally use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following reasons:

-   **[Necessary/Essential Cookies]**
    -   **Type**: Session Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are super essential to give you services available through the Website and to let you use some of its features. They help authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you asked for can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to hook you up with those services.

-   **[Cookies Policy / Acceptance Notification Cookies]**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to figure out if users have accepted the use of cookies on the Website.

-   **[Functional Cookies]**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember choices you make when you use the Website, like remembering your login deets or language preference. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you use the Website.

For more deets on the Cookies we use and your options regarding them, swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
