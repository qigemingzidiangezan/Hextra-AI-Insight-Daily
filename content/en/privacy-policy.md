### Tracking Technologies & Cookies 🍪
We totally use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity within our Service and store certain info. The tracking tech we use includes beacons, tags, and scripts. They're all about collecting and tracking info, plus juicing up and analyzing our Service. The tech we might use includes:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file dropped onto your Device. You can tell your browser to ditch all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But hey, if you don't play nice with Cookies, you might not get to use some parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to give Cookies the cold shoulder, our Service might just use them.
- **Web Beacons**: Some bits of our Service and our emails might have tiny electronic files called Web Beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These awesome files let the Company (for example) count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and are used for other cool website stats (like noting how popular a certain section is and verifying system and server integrity).

Cookies can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile Device even after you go offline, while Session Cookies totally vanish once you close your web browser.

We're using both Session and Persistent Cookies for these purposes:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Managed By**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely key to give you the services available through the Website and let you use some of its rad features. They help authenticate users and stop any sketchy use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you asked for just can't happen, and we only use these Cookies to hook you up with those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed By**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are here to figure out if users have given a nod to the use of cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed By**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember choices you make when you're cruising the Website, like remembering your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you use the Website.

For more juicy details about the Cookies we use and your options regarding Cookies, head over to our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
