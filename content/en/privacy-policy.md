When you visit our Service, or even if you're just accessing it from your mobile, we might also collect the info your browser sends our way.

### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and other similar tracking tech to keep an eye on activity within our Service and stash away some info. These tracking goodies include beacons, tags, and scripts, all used to gather and track data, helping us tweak and analyze our Service. The tech we deploy might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is just a tiny file that lands on your Device. You can totally tell your browser to reject all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's trying to send. But heads up, if you don't accept Cookies, you might miss out on some parts of our Service. Unless you've already tweaked your browser settings to say "no" to Cookies, our Service will likely use 'em.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might have tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These clever little things let the Company, for instance, count who's visited those pages or opened an email, and help with other website stats (like tracking how popular a certain section is or making sure our systems and servers are all good).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Think of it like this: a Persistent Cookie hangs around on your computer or phone even after you go offline, but a Session Cookie vanishes as soon as you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the reasons we're about to lay out below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial for giving you the services available on our Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They're super helpful for verifying who's who and stopping any sneaky, fraudulent use of user accounts. Seriously, without these Cookies, the services you're asking for just wouldn't work, and we only use 'em to deliver those specific services to you.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies simply let us know if users have already given the green light to use cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are awesome because they help us remember your choices when you're using the Website – like your login info or what language you prefer. The whole point is to give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to keep punching in your preferences every single time.

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and your options concerning them? Just swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
