### Tracking Technologies & Cookies: What's the Deal?
We use Cookies and other similar tracking tech to keep tabs on what's happening on our Service and stash away some info. These tracking goodies include things like beacons, tags, and scripts, which help us collect and track data, ultimately making our Service better and letting us analyze its usage. Some of the tech we might use are:

-   **Cookies (or Browser Cookies)**: Think of a Cookie as a tiny file that lands on your device. You've got the power to tell your browser to ditch all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's trying to get sent. But here's the catch: if you give Cookies the cold shoulder, some bits of our Service might not work for you. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to outright reject 'em, our Service will likely be using Cookies.
-   **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might have these tiny electronic files called web beacons lurking (you might also hear them called clear gifs, pixel tags, or single-pixel gifs). These little guys let us, for example, count how many users have checked out specific pages or opened an email. They're also handy for other website stats, like seeing how popular a section is or double-checking our system and server health.

Cookies come in two main flavors: "Persistent" and "Session." Persistent Cookies hang around on your computer or phone even after you've logged off, but Session Cookies vanish into thin air as soon as you shut down your browser. Poof! 💨

We're using both Session and Persistent Cookies for a few reasons, which we've laid out below:

-   **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Session Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are super important for getting you the services available on the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They help us confirm who you are and stop any shady business with user accounts. No these Cookies, no services for you! We literally only use them to make sure you get what you asked for. 😉

-   **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are like a little memory jogger, letting us know if you've given us the green light to use Cookies on the Website. ✅

-   **Functionality Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are pretty neat! They help us remember your choices when you're using the Website, like keeping your login info handy or recalling your preferred language. The whole point is to give you a more customized experience, so you don't have to keep punching in your preferences every single time. 🙌

Wanna dive deeper into the Cookies we use and your options? Head over to our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of this very Privacy Policy!
