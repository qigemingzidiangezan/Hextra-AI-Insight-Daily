### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash away certain information. The tracking technologies we use include beacons, tags, and scripts, which help us collect and track info, and also to improve and analyze our Service. The tech we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file placed on your Device. You can totally tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But, if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use some cool parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to say "no thanks" to Cookies, our Service might just use them.
- **Web Beacons**: Some sections of our Service and our emails might contain small electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These awesome files let the Company, for example, count users who have visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website stats (like checking out the popularity of a certain section and making sure the system and server are totally sound).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even when you go offline, while Session Cookies vanish the second you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following purposes:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely vital to get you the services available through the Website and to let you use some of its cool features. They help authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't be provided, and we only use them to bring you those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to spot whether users have given the thumbs-up to the use of Cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you're rocking the Website, like keeping your login deets or language preferences in mind. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of having to re-enter your preferences every single time you use the Website.

For more deets on the Cookies we use and your options when it comes to Cookies, hit up our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
