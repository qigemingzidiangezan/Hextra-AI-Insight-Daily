### Tracking Technologies & Cookies
We're all about using Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep an eye on activity on our Service and store some info. These tracking goodies – like beacons, tags, and scripts – help us collect data, track stuff, and seriously level up how we analyze and improve our Service. Some of the tech we might use includes:

-   **Cookies (aka Browser Cookies)**: Think of a Cookie as a tiny file that lands on your Device. You can totally tell your browser to ditch all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But, fair warning: if you're not cool with Cookies, some parts of our Service might not play nice. Unless you've already tweaked your browser settings to say "no thanks" to Cookies, our Service will likely be using them.
-   **Web Beacons**: So, some parts of our Service and our emails might have these tiny electronic files called web beacons (you might also hear them called clear GIFs, pixel tags, or single-pixel GIFs). They let the Company, for instance, tally up users who've checked out those pages or opened an email, plus they help with other cool website stats (like seeing how popular a section is or making sure our systems and servers are all good).

Cookies can be either 'Persistent' or 'Session' types. Persistent Cookies? They stick around on your computer or phone even after you've gone offline. Session Cookies, though, vanish the moment you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these reasons:

-   **Necessary / Essential Cookies** (the real MVPs!)
    -   **Type**: Session Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are super crucial for us to give you the services on our Website and let you use all its cool features. They're key for verifying users and stopping any sketchy account use. Seriously, without these, we can't deliver the services you're asking for, and we *only* use them for that!

-   **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These simply tell us if you've given the green light to use cookies on our Website. Easy peasy!

-   **Functionality Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These awesome Cookies help us remember your choices when you're using the Website – like your login info or language preference. The whole point is to give you a smoother, more personalized experience, so you don't have to keep typing in your preferences every single time. Handy, right?

Want more deets on the cookies we use and your options? Just swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of this Privacy Policy!
