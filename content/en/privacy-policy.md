### Tracking Technologies and Cookies

We're all about using Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and squirrel away certain info. The tracking tech we use includes beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to collect and track info, and generally beef up and analyze our Service. The tech we might use includes:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file that gets dropped onto your Device. You can tell your browser to totally reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when a Cookie is being sent. But hey, if you don't play ball with Cookies, you might miss out on some cool parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to give Cookies the boot, our Service might just be using them.
- **Web Beacons**: Some sections of our Service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files known as web beacons (also called clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and are used for other related website stats (like checking out the popularity of a certain section and making sure the system and server are totally solid).

Cookies can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies hang around on your personal computer or mobile device even when you go offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you slam shut your web browser.

We're rocking both Session and Persistent Cookies for these purposes:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely vital for hooking you up with services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They're super helpful for authenticating users and making sure no one's pulling a fast one with fraudulent account usage. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply can't be delivered, and we only use these Cookies to give you those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to figure out if users have given their OK to the use of cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you're using the Website, like remembering your login deets or your language preference. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of typing in your preferences every single time you use the Website.

For even more info on the Cookies we use and your options regarding them, zoom over to our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
