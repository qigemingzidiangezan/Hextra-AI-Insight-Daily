### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We're all about using Cookies and other neat tracking tech to keep an eye on activity within our Service and store some info. These tracking tools, like beacons, tags, and scripts, help us gather and follow data, ultimately making our Service even better for you. Here's what we might use: 👇

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies** 🍪: These are little files that land on your Device. You're in charge – tell your browser to say 'nope' to all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's trying to sneak in. But fair warning: if you block 'em all, some parts of our Service might not work perfectly. Unless your browser is set to reject them, our Service will likely be using Cookies.
- **Web Beacons** 📡: You might find tiny electronic files, called web beacons (or clear gifs, pixel tags, single-pixel gifs – fancy names for the same thing!), in some parts of our Service and emails. They help us count folks who've visited a page or opened an email. Plus, they give us cool website stats, like how popular a certain section is or if our systems are running smoothly. Sneaky, but useful!

So, Cookies can be either 'Persistent' (they stick around) or 'Session' (they disappear). Persistent Cookies hang out on your computer or mobile device even after you're offline, but Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your browser. Poof! 💨

We use both types – Session and Persistent Cookies – for these reasons: 👇

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies** 🔑
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us (that's us!)
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super important for getting you the services you expect from our Website and letting you use its cool features. They're like bouncers for your account, making sure you're really you and keeping fraudsters away. Without these, we literally can't give you the services you want, so we only use 'em for that critical stuff.

- **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Cookies** ✅
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These simply remember if you've already given a nod to our use of cookies on the Website. No need to ask you twice! 😉

- **Functionality Cookies** ✨
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These are the smart cookies that help us recall your choices when you're cruising our Website – like your login info or language preference. Their whole gig is to give you a more customized experience, so you don't have to keep typing in your preferences every single time. Handy, right?

Wanna know more about our Cookies and your choices? Head over to our Cookies Policy or check out the Cookies section in THIS Privacy Policy. Get all the details! 📖
