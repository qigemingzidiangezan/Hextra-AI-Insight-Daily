### Tracking Technologies and Cookies 🍪
We use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash away certain information. The tracking technologies we use include beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to collect and track info, plus to improve and analyze our Service. The tech we might use includes:

-   **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file placed on your Device. You can tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when a Cookie is being sent. But hey, if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use certain parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser setting to refuse Cookies, our Service might just use them.
-   **Web Beacons**: Certain sections of our Service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These files let the Company, for example, count users who have visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website stats (like noting the popularity of a certain section and verifying system and server integrity).

Cookies can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device when you go offline, while Session Cookies vanish as soon as you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the purposes outlined below:

-   **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Session Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are crucial for giving you services available through the Website and letting you use some of its features. They help authenticate users and stop fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to hook you up with those services.

-   **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies figure out if users have given the thumbs-up to the use of cookies on the Website.

-   **Functionality Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember choices you make when you use the Website, like recalling your login deets or language preference. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every time you use the Website.

For more info on the Cookies we use and your choices regarding Cookies, check out our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
