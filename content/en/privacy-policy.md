### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We're big fans of using Cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep an eye on activity on our Service and stash away certain info. The tracking technologies we deploy include beacons, tags, and scripts, all working to collect and track information, and ultimately, to make our Service even better. The tech we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file that plants itself on your Device. You can totally tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one is being sent. But hey, if you don't accept Cookies, you might miss out on some cool parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to give Cookies the boot, our Service might just use 'em. 🍪
- **Web Beacons**: Some sections of our Service and even our emails might contain these small electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company (for example) count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website stats (like checking out the popularity of a certain section and verifying system and server integrity).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even when you're offline, while Session Cookies vanish into thin air the moment you close your web browser. Poof!

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the purposes we're about to spill below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely vital to hook you up with services available through the Website and to let you use some of its features. They help authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't be delivered, and we only use these Cookies to provide you with those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are all about figuring out if users have given the thumbs-up to the use of cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super helpful! They allow us to remember the choices you make when you're cruising the Website, like recalling your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you hit up the Website.

For even more deets on the Cookies we use and your options regarding Cookies, just swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
