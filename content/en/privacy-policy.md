### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We're all about using Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep an eye on activity on our Service and stash away certain info. Think beacons, tags, and scripts – these bad boys help us collect and track data, ultimately making our Service better and giving us some sweet analytics. The tech we might use includes: 👀

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file placed on Your Device. You can tell Your browser to refuse all Cookies or to indicate when a Cookie is being sent. However, if You do not accept Cookies, You might not be able to use some parts of our Service. Unless You have adjusted Your browser setting so that it will refuse Cookies, our Service may use Cookies.
- **Web Beacons**: Certain sections of our Service and our emails may contain small electronic files known as web beacons (also referred to as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These handy files permit the Company, for example, to count users who have visited those pages or opened an email and for other related website statistics (for example, recording the popularity of a certain section and verifying system and server integrity).

So, Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Think of Persistent Cookies as the ones that stick around on your computer or phone even after you're offline. Session Cookies, though? Poof! They vanish the second you close your web browser. 💨

We actually use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the reasons listed below: 👇

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely vital to give you the services available through the Website and to let you use some of its cool features. They help authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to deliver those services to you.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are all about figuring out if users have accepted the use of cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super useful because they let us remember choices you make when you're using the Website, like recalling your login deets or language preferences. The whole point is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you visit.

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and what choices you've got? Just zip over to our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of this very Privacy Policy. 📖
