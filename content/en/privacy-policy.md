### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
Cookies and similar tracking technologies are what we use to keep tabs on activity on our service and stash away certain info. These tracking tech tools — think beacons, tags, and scripts — are all about gathering and tracing info, ultimately making our service better and helping us analyze it. What kind of tech are we talking about? Well, it might include: 🍪✨

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is just a tiny file dropped onto your device. You can totally tell your browser to reject all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But heads up: if you don't accept Cookies, some parts of our service might not play nice. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to give Cookies the boot, our service might just use 'em! 🚦
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our service and our emails might have these tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear GIFs, pixel tags, and single-pixel GIFs). These bad boys let the Company, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and are used for other cool website stats (like checking out a section's popularity and making sure systems and servers are all good). 👀

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. When you go offline, Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you slam that web browser shut. Poof! 👋

We totally use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these purposes: 👇

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial for hooking you up with the services available through the website and letting you use some of its features. They help verify who you are and prevent any funky, fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you're asking for just wouldn't happen, and we only use 'em to bring you those services. Can't live without 'em! 🛠️

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are all about remembering if you've given the green light to using Cookies on the website. So we know you're cool with it! ✅

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are awesome because they let us remember your choices when you're using the website – like keeping your login deets or language preferences in mind. The whole point? To give you a more personal experience and save you from having to re-enter your preferences every single time you visit. Sweet! 🧠

For even more info on the Cookies we use and your options regarding them, just swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of this Privacy Policy. Dig in! 📖
