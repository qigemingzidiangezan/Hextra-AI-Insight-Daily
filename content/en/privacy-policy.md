### Tracking Technologies & Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash away certain info. The tracking tech we roll with includes beacons, tags, and scripts. They're all about gathering and tracking info, and helping us fine-tune and analyze our Service. The tech we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file dropped onto your Device. You can totally tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But hey, if you don't accept Cookies, you might miss out on some cool parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to decline Cookies, our Service might just use 'em. 🍪
- **Web Beacons**: Certain sections of our Service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These bad boys let the Company, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and are used for other related website stats (like checking out a section's popularity and verifying system and server integrity).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device even when you go offline, while Session Cookies get zapped as soon as you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the purposes we're about to lay out below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial for dishing out services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They're all about authenticating users and preventing any sneaky, fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for just can't happen, and we only use 'em to bring those services to you. 🚀

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to figure out if users have given the green light to the use of Cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are pretty neat! They let us remember choices you make when you're using the Website, like recalling your login deets or language preference. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of having to re-enter your preferences every single time you visit. ✨

For more info on the Cookies we use and your choices regarding 'em, swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
