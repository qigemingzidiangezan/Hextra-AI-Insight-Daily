### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash away certain info. The tracking technologies we use include beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to collect and track info, and to improve and analyze our Service. The tech we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a small file dropped on your device. You can tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when a Cookie is being sent. But hey, if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use some parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser setting to refuse Cookies, our Service might just use 'em.
- **Web Beacons**: Certain sections of our Service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These awesome files let the Company (for example) count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website stats (like, recording the popularity of a certain section and double-checking system and server integrity).

Cookies can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even when you're offline, while Session Cookies get the boot the moment you close your web browser.

We rock both Session and Persistent Cookies for these purposes:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely essential to hook you up with services available through the Website and to let you use some of its cool features. They help authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to deliver those services to you.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to figure out if users have accepted the use of cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you use the Website, like remembering your login deets or language preference. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every time you hit up the Website.

For more juicy info about the Cookies we use and your options regarding cookies, give our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy a gander.
