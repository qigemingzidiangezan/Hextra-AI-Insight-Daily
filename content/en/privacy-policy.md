### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We totally use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on what's happening on our Service and to stash away some info. We're talking beacons, tags, and scripts – all used to gather and track data, plus help us level up and analyze our Service. The tech we might use includes: 👇

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is just a tiny file that lands on your Device. You can totally tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But, heads-up: if you don't accept Cookies, some parts of our Service might not work for you. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to give Cookies the cold shoulder, our Service will likely be using them.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might have these tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). They're pretty neat because they let us, for example, count how many users have checked out those pages or opened an email. Plus, they're super handy for other website stats, like seeing how popular a section is or just making sure our system and servers are running smoothly. 👍

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent ones stick around on your computer or mobile device even after you're offline. Session Cookies, though, vanish as soon as you close your web browser. Poof! They're gone! 💨

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the purposes set out below: 👇

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super crucial for us to actually give you the services available on the Website and let you use some of its cool features. They help us verify who you are and stop any shady, fraudulent use of user accounts. Honestly, without these Cookies, the services you're asking for just wouldn't happen, so we only use them to make sure you get those services. No funny business! 🔒

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies simply let us know if you've given a nod to using Cookies on our Website. It's like a little digital handshake! 🤝

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are like your personal memory assistant! 🧠 They let us remember the choices you make when you're using the Website, like keeping your login info handy or remembering your language preference. The whole point is to give you a more personalized experience, saving you from having to punch in your preferences every single time you visit. Sweet! 😎

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and what choices you have? Head over to our Cookies Policy or just check out the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy. We've got all the deets there! 📚
