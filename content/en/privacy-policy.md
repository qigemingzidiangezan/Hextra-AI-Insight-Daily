### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
Alright, let's talk tracking tech! 🍪 We use Cookies and similar technologies to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash some info. These tracking tools — think beacons, tags, and scripts — help us gather and track data, plus beef up and analyze our Service. Here's a peek at what we use:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: These are small files popped onto your Device. You can tell your browser to ditch all Cookies or alert you when one's coming. But heads up, if you don't take Cookies, some parts of our Service might not play nice. Unless you've set your browser to refuse 'em, our Service will likely use Cookies.
- **Web Beacons**: Picture tiny electronic files, sometimes called clear gifs, pixel tags, or single-pixel gifs, lurking in some parts of our Service and emails. These 'Web Beacons' help us, for instance, tally up users who've checked out those pages or opened an email, and gather other cool website stats (like seeing how popular a section is or making sure our systems are solid).

Cookies come in two flavors: "Persistent" or "Session." Persistent Cookies chill on your device even after you go offline, but Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for a few reasons, check 'em out below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These are the MVPs (Most Valuable Cookies) for getting you the services available on our Website and letting you rock its features. They're all about authenticating users and keeping fraudsters out of your account. No these Cookies, no fun services! We only use them to get you what you asked for.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These help us remember if you've already given us the thumbs up for using cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These clever Cookies let us remember your choices when you're using the Website – think login details or your favorite language. They're here to give you a smoother, more personalized experience, so you don't have to re-enter your preferences every single time.

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and your options? Just hit up our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section in this Privacy Policy.
