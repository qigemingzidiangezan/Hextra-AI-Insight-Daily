### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We totally use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our Service and stash away some info. These goodies include beacons, tags, and scripts – all to collect and track info, then use it to boost and analyze our Service. Here's what we might be using:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is just a tiny file that lands on your Device. You can totally tell your browser to reject all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But, fair warning: if you skip the Cookies, some parts of our Service might not work for you. Unless you've already tweaked your browser settings to say "no thanks" to Cookies, our Service will likely be using them.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and even our emails might have these tiny digital files called web beacons (you might also hear them called clear GIFs, pixel tags, or single-pixel GIFs). These help the Company, for instance, count how many users swung by those pages or opened an email, and gather other cool website stats (like seeing how popular a section is or making sure our systems are all good).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" ones. Persistent Cookies stick around on your computer or mobile even after you go offline, but Session Cookies vanish the second you close the web browser. Poof! 💨

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these reasons:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies** 🔑
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial for us to even offer you the services on the Website and let you use some of its cool features. They're like bouncers, helping to verify users and stop any shady, fraudulent account use. Without 'em, the services you're asking for just can't happen, and we only use these Cookies to make sure you get those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies** ✅
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are basically a thumbs-up detector, checking if you've already given the green light to using Cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies** ✨
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are pretty neat! They let us remember the choices you make while using the Website, like your login info or what language you prefer. The whole point is to give you a more personalized vibe and save you the hassle of typing in your preferences every single time you visit.

Want to know more about the Cookies we use and your choices? Just head over to our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
