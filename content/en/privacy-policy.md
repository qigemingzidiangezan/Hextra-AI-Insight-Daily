### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We totally use Cookies and other similar tracking tech to keep an eye on activity on our Service and stash away some info. These tracking goodies include beacons, tags, and scripts – all used to collect, track info, and generally make our Service better and more insightful. 🕵️‍♀️ The tech we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is just a tiny file chillin' on your Device. You can totally tell your browser to reject all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But heads up: if you don't accept Cookies, you might miss out on some cool parts of our Service. Unless you've already tweaked your browser settings to say 'no thanks' to Cookies, our Service will likely be using them.
- **Web Beacons**: You know those tiny electronic files sometimes hidden in our Service sections and emails? They're called web beacons (or clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs!). These little guys let the Company, for instance, count who's visited pages or opened an email, and gather other website stats (like how popular a section is or just making sure our systems are running smoothly). ✨

So, Cookies can be either 'Persistent' or 'Session' Cookies. The Persistent ones? They hang around on your computer or mobile device even after you're offline. Session Cookies, though, poof! They're gone as soon as you close your web browser. 👋

We totally use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the reasons we're about to lay out below: 👇

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are those super important ones. Their purpose is totally crucial for getting you the services available through the Website and letting you use some of its cool features. They help verify user identities and seriously block any sketchy, fraudulent use of user accounts. No these Cookies, no services you've asked for! We only use these specific Cookies to hook you up with those services. 😎

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to identify if users have given us the thumbs-up on using cookies on the Website. 👍

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are all about remembering the choices you make while using the Website – things like your login info or what language you prefer. The whole point of these is to give you a super personalized experience and save you the hassle of typing in your preferences every single time. How convenient! ✨

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and what your options are? Just hop over to our Cookies Policy or check out the Cookies section in this very Privacy Policy. 🍪
