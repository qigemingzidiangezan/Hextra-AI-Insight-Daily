### Tracking Technologies & Cookies
We totally use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep tabs on activity on our service and to store certain information. The tracking tech we use includes beacons, tags, and scripts. They help us collect and track info, plus improve and analyze our service. Here's what we might be using:

-   **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file dropped onto your device. You can tell your browser to say "nope!" 🚫 to all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But heads up: if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use some cool parts of our service. And unless you've tweaked your browser settings to reject 'em, our service might just use Cookies.
-   **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files called Web Beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These awesome files let the Company count users who've visited those pages or opened an email. They're also used for other related website statistics (like checking out the popularity of a certain section and making sure the system and server are running smoothly).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even when you go offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for these purposes:

-   **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Session Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are a *must-have* to provide you with the services available through the Website and to enable you to use some of its coolest features. They help authenticate users and prevent tricky, fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to give you those services. No kidding!
-   **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to spot if users have given a nod to the use of cookies on the Website. ✅
-   **Functionality Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Administered by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you use the Website. Think remembering your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you use the Website.

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and your options regarding them? Go check out our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy. It's all there!
