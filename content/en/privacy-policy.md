And hey, whether you're on desktop or mobile, we might also scoop up info that your browser sends our way.

### Tracking Tech & Cookies 🍪

So, about **Cookies** and other fancy **tracking technologies**? We totally use 'em! They help us keep tabs on what's happening on our Service and stash away certain info. We're talking beacons, tags, and scripts – all for collecting, tracking, and ultimately making our Service even better for you. Here's a peek at what we might use:

- **Cookies (or Browser Cookies, as some call 'em)**: What's a cookie? Just a tiny file plonked on your device. You can totally tell your browser to reject all cookies or give you a heads-up when one's trying to sneak in. But heads up: if you don't play nice with cookies, some parts of our Service might not work perfectly. Unless you've fiddled with your browser settings to say 'no' to cookies, our Service will likely be using them.
- **Web Beacons**: Ever wonder how we know if you opened an email or visited a page? Some parts of our Service and our emails might have these tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). They let us (for example) count who's visited those pages or opened emails, and they're super handy for other website stats, like seeing which parts are popular or checking if our systems are running smoothly.

Alright, so cookies come in two main flavors: **'Persistent'** and **'Session'** Cookies. The 'Persistent' ones stick around on your computer or mobile device even after you've gone offline. But 'Session' Cookies? Poof! They vanish the second you close your browser. ✨

Why do we use both Session and Persistent Cookies? We've got our reasons, spelled out right below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies** 🔑
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These cookies are an absolute must-have for getting you the services available through the Website and letting you use all its cool features. They're key for authenticating users and keeping those pesky fraudulent accounts at bay. Seriously, without 'em, the services you ask for just wouldn't happen. We only use these to deliver what you need!

- **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Cookies** ✅
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These handy cookies simply remember if you've already given us the nod to use cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies** 🧠
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Managed by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These cookies are super smart! They let us remember your choices when you're using the Website—things like your login deets or what language you prefer. The goal? To give you a more personalized experience so you don't have to keep punching in your preferences every single time you visit.

Wanna dive deeper into the cookies we use and your options? Just hit up our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section in this very Privacy Policy!
