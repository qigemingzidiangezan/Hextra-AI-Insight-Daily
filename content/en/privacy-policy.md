### Tracking Technologies & Cookies
We use Cookies and other similar tracking tech to keep an eye on activity on our service and store some info. The tracking technologies we deploy include beacons, tags, and scripts. They help us collect and track data, plus they're super useful for improving and analyzing our service. Some of these technologies might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is just a small file chilling on your device. You can totally tell your browser to reject all Cookies or give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But, heads up, if you don't accept Cookies, you might miss out on some cool parts of our service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to say "no thanks" to Cookies, our service will likely use them.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our service and even our emails might have these tiny electronic files called web beacons (you might also know them as clear GIFs, pixel tags, or single-pixel GIFs). These little guys let us, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email. They're also used for other website stats, like tracking how popular a section is or just making sure our systems and servers are all good.

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device even after you go offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser. Poof!

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the reasons listed below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super important for making the website's services available to you and letting you use some of its cool features. They help verify who's who and stop any sneaky fraudulent use of accounts. Without these Cookies, we just can't deliver the services you've asked for, and yep, we only use them for exactly that.

- **Cookies Policy / Notification Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies simply help us remember if you've given the green light to use Cookies on our website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are neat because they let us remember the choices you make while using the website. Think remembering your login info or what language you prefer. Their goal is to give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to punch in your preferences every single time you visit. Sweet!

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and what your options are? Just hop over to our Cookies Policy or check out the Cookies section in this very Privacy Policy.
