### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to monitor activity on our Service and store certain information. The tracking technologies we employ include beacons, tags, and scripts, all used to collect and track information, as well as to improve and analyze our Service. The technologies we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A tiny file is placed on your Device. You can tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or to indicate when a Cookie is being sent. However, if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use some parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser setting to refuse Cookies, our Service might just use 'em.
- **Web Beacons**: Certain sections of our Service and our emails might contain small electronic files known as web beacons (also referred to as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These handy files allow the Company, for example, to count users who have visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website statistics (e.g., recording the popularity of a certain section and verifying system and server integrity).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies stay on your personal computer or mobile device when you go offline, whereas Session Cookies get deleted the moment you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the purposes outlined below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial to give you the services available through the Website and to let you use some of its cool features. They help authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to deliver those services to you.

- **Cookies Policy / Notification Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are used to identify whether users have accepted the use of cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you use the Website, like recalling your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you use the Website.

For more info about the Cookies we use and your choices regarding Cookies, check out our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
