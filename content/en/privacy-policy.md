### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We're all about using Cookies and other similar tracking tech to keep tabs on what's happening on our Service and to stash away some info. We're talking beacons, tags, and scripts – all to gather and track data, helping us spruce up and understand our Service better. The tech we use could include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies:** Think of a Cookie as a tiny file chilling on your device. You can tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's trying to sneak in. But hey, if you don't play ball with Cookies, some parts of our Service might not work for you. Unless you've explicitly told your browser to ditch Cookies, our Service will likely be using them.
- **Web Beacons:** You might find tiny electronic files, sometimes called web beacons (or clear gifs, pixel tags, single-pixel gifs – fancy names, right?), hiding in some parts of our Service and emails. These help us, for instance, count how many folks have visited certain pages or opened an email and are also great for other website stats, like seeing which sections are popular or just checking if our systems are running smoothly.

Cookies come in two main flavors: "Persistent" or "Session." Persistent Cookies stick around on your personal computer or mobile device even after you've gone offline, while Session Cookies? Poof! They vanish the moment you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies, and here's why:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type:** Session Cookies
  - **Administered by:** Us
  - **Purpose:** These Cookies are super important for getting you the services available on our Website and letting you use some cool features. They're like our bouncers, helping to confirm who you are and stopping any sneaky, fraudulent account usage. Seriously, without these, you wouldn't get the services you asked for, and We *only* use them for that very reason.

- **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type:** Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by:** Us
  - **Purpose:** These Cookies simply remember if you've already given the thumbs-up to our use of cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type:** Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by:** Us
  - **Purpose:** These Cookies are pretty handy! They help us remember choices You make when you're cruising around the Website, like your login info or what language you prefer. Basically, they're here to give you a more personal experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you visit.

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and what your options are? Just hit up our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of this very Privacy Policy.
