### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep tabs on activity on our Service and squirrel away certain info. The tracking tech we use includes beacons, tags, and scripts – all designed to gather and track info, plus improve and analyze our Service. The tech we might employ could be:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is a tiny file dropped onto your Device. You can totally tell your browser to refuse all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But, heads up, if you don't accept Cookies, you might not be able to use some cool parts of our Service. Unless you've tweaked your browser settings to give Cookies the cold shoulder, our Service might just use 'em.
- **Web Beacons**: Certain sections of our Service and our emails might contain tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear GIFs, pixel tags, and single-pixel GIFs). These bad boys let the Company (for instance) count users who've visited those pages or opened an email, and for other related website stats (like, logging the popularity of a certain section and double-checking system and server integrity). Pretty neat, huh?

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill out on your personal computer or mobile device even when you're offline, while Session Cookies get the boot the moment you close your web browser. Poof! 👋

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following reasons:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial to hook you up with services available through the Website and to let you use some of its cool features. They help us verify users and prevent any funky, fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for simply can't be provided, and we only use 'em to deliver those services to you. So yeah, they're kind of a big deal! 😎

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are all about figuring out if users have given the green light to the use of cookies on the Website. It's like a digital handshake. 🤝

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you're cruising around the Website, like keeping your login deets or language preferences in mind. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you use the Website. Sweet, right? ✨

For even more juicy details about the Cookies we use and your choices regarding them, go ahead and visit our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy. Get informed! 🤓
