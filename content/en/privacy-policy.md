## Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

### Types of Data We Collect

#### Personal Data
When You're using our Service, we might ask You for some personal info that helps us get in touch or figure out who You are. This personally identifiable stuff could include, but isn't limited to:

- Email address
- Usage Data

#### Usage Data
Usage Data? That's the stuff we automatically collect whenever You're using the Service.

Usage Data might include goodies like Your Device's Internet Protocol address (aka IP address), what browser You're using, its version, which pages of our Service You checked out, when You visited, how long You hung around, Your device's unique ID, and other techy diagnostic info.

Hitting up the Service on Your mobile device? We might automatically snag some info, like what kind of phone You're rocking, its unique ID, its IP address, Your mobile OS, the type of mobile browser You're using, unique device identifiers, and other diagnostic bits.

And hey, when You visit our Service (on desktop or mobile), we might also grab the info Your browser sends our way.

### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We use Cookies and other savvy tracking tech to keep tabs on what's happening on our Service and to stash away some info. We're talking beacons, tags, and scripts—all there to gather and track data, helping us fine-tune and analyze our Service. The tech we use might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: Think of a Cookie as a tiny file tucked onto Your Device. You're the boss: You can tell Your browser to reject all Cookies or to give You a heads-up when one's trying to sneak in. But fair warning: if You ditch Cookies, some parts of our Service might not play nice. Unless You've told Your browser to flat-out refuse them, our Service will likely be using Cookies.
- **Web Beacons**: Some parts of our Service and our emails might have these tiny electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, or single-pixel gifs). They let us, for instance, count who's visited those pages or opened an email, and help with other website stats (like seeing how popular a section is or checking if our systems and servers are all good).

Cookies can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent ones stick around on Your computer or phone even after You've gone offline, but Session Cookies vanish the moment You close Your web browser. Poof!

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the reasons we're about to lay out below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super crucial for us to actually give You the services on the Website and let You use some of its cool features. They're like bouncers, helping us verify who's who and stopping any funny business with user accounts. No these Cookies, no services for You—plain and simple! We only use them to make sure You get what You asked for.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are basically our way of remembering if You've given the thumbs-up to Cookies on our Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are our little helpers, letting us remember Your choices when You're using the Website—stuff like Your login deets or what language You prefer. The whole point? To give You a super personalized experience so You don't have to keep punching in Your preferences every single time You visit.

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and what Your options are? Just zip over to our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section in this very Privacy Policy!
