### Tracking Technologies & Cookies 🍪
We're totally using Cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep tabs on what's happening on our Service and stash away certain bits of info. The tracking tech we use includes beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to collect and track information, plus help us perk up and analyze our Service. The tech we deploy might include:

-   **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is just a tiny file that gets placed on your device. You can totally tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a nudge when one's being sent. Heads up though, if you don't accept Cookies, you might miss out on some parts of our Service. Unless you've already fiddled with your browser settings to reject 'em, our Service will likely be using Cookies.
-   **Web Beacons**: Some corners of our Service and our emails might have small electronic files called Web Beacons (also known as clear gifs, pixel tags, and single-pixel gifs). These little guys let the Company, for instance, count who's visited those pages or opened an email, and help with other website stats (like seeing how popular a section is and making sure systems and servers are all good to go).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies just chill on your personal computer or mobile device even after you're offline, while Session Cookies totally poof away the second you close your web browser. 💨

We're using both Session and Persistent Cookies for these cool reasons:

-   **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Session Cookies
    -   **Managed by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are absolutely crucial for hooking you up with the services available through the Website and letting you use some of its awesome functions. They're key to verifying users and putting a stop to any shady use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you're asking for simply can't happen, and we only use them to deliver those specific services to you.

-   **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Notice Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Managed by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are here to figure out if users have given the thumbs-up to using Cookies on the Website. 👍

-   **Functionality Cookies**
    -   **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    -   **Managed by**: Us
    -   **Purpose**: These Cookies are super handy because they let us remember your choices when you're using the Website. Think: remembering your login deets or your language preferences. Their whole vibe is to give you a more personalized experience, so you don't have to re-enter your preferences every single time you visit the Website.

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and what your choices are? Just hop over to our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
