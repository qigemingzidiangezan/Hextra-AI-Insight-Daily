### Tracking Technologies and Cookies 🍪
We use Cookies and similar tracking tech to keep tabs on activity on our service and stash away certain info. The tracking technologies we use include beacons, tags, and scripts, all designed to collect and track info, plus improve and analyze our service. Our tech might include:

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies:** A Cookie is a tiny file nestled on your device. You can tell your browser to give all Cookies the boot or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But here's the kicker: if you don't accept Cookies, some parts of our service might not play nice. Our service might still use Cookies unless you've tweaked your browser settings to reject 'em.
- **Web Beacons:** Some parts of our service and our emails might contain small electronic files called web beacons (also known as clear GIFs, pixel tags, and single-pixel GIFs). These files let the Company, for example, count users who've visited those pages or opened an email. They're also used for other related website stats (like checking out how popular a certain section is and verifying system and server integrity).

Cookies can be either "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies chill on your personal computer or mobile device even when you go offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment you close your web browser.

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the following reasons:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type:** Session Cookies
  - **Administered by:** Us
  - **Purpose:** These Cookies are super crucial for dishing out the services available through our Website and letting you use some of its features. They help authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services you've asked for can't be provided, and we only use these Cookies to give you those services.

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type:** Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by:** Us
  - **Purpose:** These Cookies figure out if users have given the green light to the use of cookies on the Website.

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type:** Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by:** Us
  - **Purpose:** These Cookies let us remember the choices you make when you're using the Website, like recalling your login deets or language preferences. The whole point of these Cookies is to give you a more personalized experience, saving you from having to re-enter your preferences every time you use the Website.

For more info about the Cookies we use and your options regarding them, swing by our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
