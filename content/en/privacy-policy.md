### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
Alright, let's talk Cookies! 🍪 We use them and other cool tracking tech to keep tabs on what's happening on our Service and store a bit of info. We're talking beacons, tags, and scripts here – all for gathering data, tracking activity, and ultimately making our Service even better for You. Our tech toolkit includes:

- **Cookies (or Browser Cookies, if You're fancy)**: Picture a tiny file that lands on Your Device. You can totally tell Your browser to refuse all Cookies or give You a heads-up when one's being sent. But, heads-up: if You don't accept 'em, some parts of our Service might play hard to get. Unless You have specifically told Your browser to deny Cookies, our Service will likely be using them.

- **Web Beacons**: Ever heard of these? They're tiny electronic files (sometimes called clear gifs, pixel tags, or single-pixel gifs) found in some parts of our Service and our emails. They let the Company do cool things like count how many users have visited certain pages or opened an email, and help us with other website stats (like checking how popular a section is or ensuring our systems are running smoothly).

So, Cookies come in two main flavors: "Persistent" and "Session." Persistent Cookies stick around on Your personal computer or mobile device even after You've gone offline, while Session Cookies vanish the moment You close Your web browser. Poof! 💨

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for a few specific reasons, which we've laid out below:

- **Necessary / Essential Cookies**
  - **Type**: Session Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These Cookies are super crucial for making sure You can actually *use* our Website and all its cool features. They're like the bouncers for user authentication, helping us prevent any dodgy business with user accounts. Without these, the services that You're asking for just can't happen, so We only use them to get You what You need!

- **Cookies Policy / Acceptance Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These little guys simply remember if users have already given us the green light on using Cookies on the Website. Saves You from seeing that banner every time! ✅

- **Functionality Cookies**
  - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
  - **Administered by**: Us
  - **Purpose**: These are the smart Cookies! They help us remember choices You make when You're using the Website, like Your login deets or what language You prefer. The goal? To give You a more personal experience so You don't have to re-enter Your preferences every single time. Handy, right?

Want even more deets on the Cookies we use and what choices You have? No sweat! Just swing by our Cookies Policy or hit up the Cookies section of our Privacy Policy.
