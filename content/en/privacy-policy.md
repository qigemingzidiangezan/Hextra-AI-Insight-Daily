### Tracking Technologies and Cookies
We're all about using Cookies and other similar tracking tech to keep tabs on what's happening on our Service and to stash away some info. We're talkin' beacons, tags, and scripts – the whole shebang – to gather and track data, helping us seriously level up and analyze our Service. Here's a peek at the tech we might be using: 👇

- **Cookies or Browser Cookies**: A Cookie is just a tiny file that gets plonked onto your Device. You've got the power to tell your browser to reject all Cookies or to give you a heads-up when one's being sent. But here's the catch: if you don't play nice with Cookies, you might miss out on some awesome parts of our Service. So, unless you've set your browser to give Cookies the cold shoulder, our Service might just be using 'em! 🍪

- **Web Beacons**: Get this – some parts of our Service and even our emails might have these tiny electronic files called web beacons (you might also hear 'em called clear GIFs, pixel tags, or single-pixel GIFs). These little guys let the Company, for example, tally up users who've visited certain pages or opened an email. They also help with other cool website stats, like tracking how popular a section is or making sure our systems and servers are totally solid. Pretty neat, huh? ✨

So, Cookies can either be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. The persistent ones stick around on your computer or phone even after you go offline. Session Cookies, though? Poof! They vanish the second you close your web browser. 💨

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the purposes set out below:

- **Necessary/Essential Cookies** 🤩
    - **Type**: Session Cookies
    - **Administered by**: Us
    - **Purpose**: These Cookies are an absolute must-have to give you the services available through the Website and let you use some of its cool features. They're super helpful for verifying who's who and stopping any sketchy, fraudulent use of user accounts. Honestly, without these, the services you're asking for just wouldn't work, and we *only* use 'em to deliver those services to you!

- **Cookies Policy / Notice Acceptance Cookies** ✅
    - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    - **Administered by**: Us
    - **Purpose**: These Cookies are all about figuring out if you've given us the thumbs-up to use Cookies on the Website. Simple as that!

- **Functionality Cookies** 🧠
    - **Type**: Persistent Cookies
    - **Administered by**: Us
    - **Purpose**: These Cookies are like our little memory banks! They let us remember the choices you make when you're using the Website, like your login info or what language you prefer. The whole point? To give you a super personalized experience and save you the hassle of re-entering your preferences every single time you visit. Sweet deal, right?

Wanna know more about the Cookies we use and what choices you've got? No sweat! Just head over to our Cookies Policy or the Cookies section tucked away in this Privacy Policy. All the deets are there! 📖
