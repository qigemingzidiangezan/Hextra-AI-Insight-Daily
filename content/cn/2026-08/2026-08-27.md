3.  **Anthropic**：与基础设施提供商Nscale达成450亿美元协议，持续扩大其计算能力投入。💰 [原文链接](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/26/anthropic-continues-compute-gobbling-streak-in-45-billion-deal-with-nscale/)
4.  **OpenAI**：发布关于Hugging Face数据泄露事件的官方报告，详细说明了多起网络安全漏洞事件。🔒 [原文链接](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/26/openai-releases-its-official-report-on-the-hugging-face-breach/)

7.  **谷歌Gemini**：更新音频转录功能，可自动检测并编辑掉口头禅，如“嗯”和“啊”。✍️ [原文链接](https://www.theverge.com/tech/985186/google-gemini-3-5-transcribe-audio-ai)
