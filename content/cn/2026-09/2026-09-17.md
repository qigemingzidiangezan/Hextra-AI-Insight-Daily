## **AI内容摘要**
今日AI领域动态聚焦于AI安全与治理，Anthropic和OpenAI计划引入内部安全评估员，前副总统Al Gore亦强调AI技术走向的深层风险。在产品层面，Google Home开放AI代理控制智能家居，Anthropic整合Claude界面，Snap推出全新AI助手。此外，AI数据中心带来的电子垃圾问题及Apple可能重返服务器市场，凸显了AI基础设施建设的挑战与机遇。

## **AI产品与功能更新**

1.  **Google Home**: **Google Home** 现已开放早期访问，允许 **Claude**、**ChatGPT** 等AI代理通过自然语言控制智能家居设备，查看摄像头摘要及活动。🏡 [https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/16/your-ai-agents-can-now-control-your-google-home-devices/](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/16/your-ai-agents-can-now-control-your-google-home-devices/)

2.  **Anthropic Claude**: **Anthropic** 将 **Claude** 聊天与 **Cowork** 功能整合至统一界面，面向Pro和Max订阅用户推出，提升用户体验。✨ [https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/16/anthropic-merges-claude-chat-and-cowork-in-one-interface/](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/16/anthropic-merges-claude-chat-and-cowork-in-one-interface/)

3.  **Snap Specs AI**: **Snap** 推出 **Specs Intelligence** AI助手，可连接其他数字账户，协助用户处理工作任务和管理旅行信息，即将登陆iOS和Mac。👓 [https://www.theverge.com/tech/996078/snap-specs-intelligence-ai-agent-ios-mac](https://www.theverge.com/tech/996078/snap-specs-intelligence-ai-agent-ios-mac)

4.  **Meta (Ray-Ban)**: **Meta** 计划推出一款不带摄像头的新款智能眼镜，以回应此前“偷窥眼镜”的指控，尝试改善用户隐私担忧。🕶️ [https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/16/after-accusations-of-selling-perv-glasses-meta-prepares-to-sell-a-pair-without-a-camera/](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/16/after-accusations-of-selling-perv-glasses-meta-prepares-to-sell-a-pair-without-a-camera/)
